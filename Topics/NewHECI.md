## Yet Another Empowered Committee on Implementation of HECI 

There is a [small news item on report of Radhakrishnan led committee](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/isro-ex-chief-led-panel-to-onboard-heis-in-new-regulatory-setup/articleshow/121998309.cms) on formation of a committee for recommending reforms in Higher Educational Institutions in India. [Dr. K. Radhakrishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koppillil_Radhakrishnan#:~:text=K.,of%20Space%2C%20Government%20of%20India.) was ISRO chairman and Chairman Board of Governers of IIT Kanpur. He is basically a remote sensing expert, and instrumental in setting up of a chain of Regional Remote Sensing Servicing Centers at Bangalore, Jodhpur, Nagpur and Dehradun. RRSSCs are aimed at generation of spatial data about resources. The purpose of mentioning his background is to aprise the reader about his credentials to lead a team of experts on education. The HEI committee which Dr Krishanan heds comprises 18 persons including the Directors of IIT-Bombay, IIM-Ahmedabad, School of Planning and Architect, New Delhi and VCs of Delhi, Utkal and Sastra University, Tanjavur. It will also include Chairpersons of UGC, AICTE and NCTE. Dr. Radhakrishanan seems to have operated as Chairperson of a number of high powered committee and established his credentials to lead such ensemble of people/experts with diverse and highly opinionated views. So, I think the news about formation of such a committee deserves more than a fly reading.  

The committee is tasked with resolving implementation issues and harmonizing existing protocols under aegis of new overarching centralized regulatory body called HECI. The committee is supposed to submit its report within a timeframe of three weeks. It is indeed commendable to note that Dr. Radhakrishanan for having accepted the daunting task of on boarding ensemble of experts having over 400 years of cummulative practical experience in Higher Education system. He will have to summon his extra-terristerial experience as the erstwhile chairperson of ISRO for reaching a covergence of opinion and suggestions. Or am I missing the key point that perhaps a report of some sort already exists and the committee is just a time stamping mechanism!  

It is no brainer to understand, our HEI regulatory bodies reek of corruption and incompetence, both in good measures. It starts with appointment of head of the body and percolate down with totally indifferent, incompetent and a majority of corrupt highly politicised employees. We don't have decent institutions beyond IITs, NIITs, CFTIs, a few well established private institutions like Ashoka and BITS. It is an irony and shame that Indian students go to countries like Bangladesh, Iran, Ukraine, Nepal, China for medical studies. Everytime a major armed conflict arises anywhere across the globe, Indian government has to undertake a major evacuation operation to save Indians. Perhaps it will be better to invest heavily in human resources inside the country than to be prepare to a evacuation operation. 

NAAC, AICTE and UGC all have their share of inglorious past and . The scandal involving NAAC assessment experts is fresh in our minds. CBI raided and rounded scores of experts including a professor of JNU in the scandal.
