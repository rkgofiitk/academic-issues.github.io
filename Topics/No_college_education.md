## Degrees Versus Education and Knowledge

Earning a degree empowers success in job hunting. However, to remain employed and be useful college degree has little relevance. The skill requirements for remain employed are different from what is taught at schools or colleges. It explains the need for continuous evolution of pedagogic approach to knowledge transfers. Plato makes a distinction between knowledge and wisdom. Knowledge empowers students to say things in order, but wisdom empowers students to think correctly. Plato describes Sophists as "merchants of knowledge who trade pedagogy for a fee, without concern for wisdom or truth.” Unfortunately, education system world over is based on the idea of knowledge transfer. The Zoho founder Sreedhat Vembu stressed no degree requirement for a job. It reinforces Plato's belief about wisdom and teaching.
