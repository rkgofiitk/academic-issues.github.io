# Rebooting Software Industry

[Blog Index](../index.md)

Indian IT industry is undergoing a serius slump. TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Mindtree, HCL all reported losses in Q4. The losses by all these company are disappointing, and things are not looking good. Many have started tech lay offs though not yet in alarming proportions. The implications of financial losses may eventually translate into long-term hiring freeze and layoffs. It is important for IT companies to reboot and look for causes of financial losses. The tech companies should look beyond the minor disruptions due AI coding assistants and US tariff hikes. US trariff may affect only marginally. Competitively, Indian IT companies are better placed as US tariff is relative low for India and has been paused for 90 days from April 15. Of course, there is still an uncertainity, but exports may benefit in the intervening period.
