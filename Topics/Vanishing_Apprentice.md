
## The Vanishing Apprentice: Risks of a Senior Engineer Shortage
[Blog Index](../index.md)

Alex Wright, a Brooklyn-based author, published an article, "[Vanishing Apprentice](https://cacm.acm.org/news/the-vanishing-apprentice/)," in the September issue of CACM that highlights issues relevant to industry professionals and reiterates points raised about the impact of coding assistants on workforce development.

Although the primary focus of Alex Wright's article is the shrinking opportunities for engineering interns, industry leaders should be concerned about a long-term shortage of senior engineers. Eventually, it may threaten industry stability and highlight the need for proactive workforce planning.

Citing the [SignalFire 2025 State of Talent Report–2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025), Wright says that the job offers for Apprentice Engineers have dropped by 50%. Worldwide, IT services companies such as Infosys, Wipro, MindTree, and Tech Mahindra are already facing declining market sentiment, with their equities down 28-29% in 2026.  It forces them to cut back recruitment massively and lay off many fresh engineers. The domino effect will create a further shortage in senior-level experts.
