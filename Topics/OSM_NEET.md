## CBSE OSM. NEET, and Reforms of the Bureaucracy

[Blog Index](../index.md)

A total of 17,68,968 students appeared in CBSE's Plus 2 exams 2026. The Board severely botched up its attempts to create transparency in reevaluation requests through the On-Screen Marking (OSM) system. It awarded a contract to a Hyderabad-based company, Coempt Edu Teck, to implement OSM. In an earlier post, we described the poor oversight and circumstances that led to the award of the contract to a company with a dubious track record, rather than to TCS. Subsequently, CBSE appointed a panel of experts from IIT Madras and IIT Kanpur to troubleshoot OSM implementation, under the leadership of Prof Kamokoti of IIT Madras. 

The retooled OSM opened for reevaluation on June 2, 2026. Soon after, it experienced a massive surge in traffic within two minutes. There were [1.5 million hits and over 14000 concurrent users](https://indianexpress.com/article/education/cbse-osm-row-portal-attack-was-a-coordinated-two-pronged-operation-says-cybersecurity-expert-osm-12th-result-10721277/). The attack was coordinated and smoke-screened as a denial-of-service attack to gain unauthorized access to files. A coordinated attack cannot be orchestrated without AI tools such as Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT. So, it is hardly surprising that [the IIT expert panel](https://www.firstpost.com/tech/iit-led-probe-finds-claude-and-other-ai-tools-behind-cbse-cyber-attack-report-ws-e-14018614.html) reached the same conclusion, saying that Anthropic's Claude is the primary AI tool used to mount the cyberattacks on the OSM site. The panel also concluded that Coempt Edu Teck lacks the expertise to counter tools or prevent cyberattacks. Incidentally, TCS has multi-dimensional capabilities to counter cyberattacks. I am neither an ex-employee nor a consultant to TCS at any point in time. However, as TCS was a competing bidder for the OSM implementation, it is natural to compare the two's capabilities. The TCS cyberattack mitigation framework includes its own cybersecurity architecture and Google Security Operations (a native cloud-specific AI-driven Intelligent security platform). However, there is no guarantee that TCS has deployed the full security architecture framework for the OSM implementation. 

The Minister of Education was quick to accept responsibility for OSM's bungling. However, the same strategy is evident in the NEET 2026 paper leak. Clubbing the two unrelated exam issues may sound a bit surprising to many. However, there is a pattern of bungling and quickly capitulating to accept responsibility in order to escape public sentiment against the government's handling of education administration. 

CBSE has had a relatively incident-free existence so far as exams and the publication of results are concerned. However, NTA has been mired in controversy from its inception. The latest event of using [IAF to distribute the NEET retest paper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2okyZWFPJr4&t=1) is not only overkill but a bad precedent. As I have suggested, a careful [implementation of CBT for NEET](./OSM_Fiasco.md) is a good approach. We have tested and validated the SoP for GATE exams. So, there is no reason why it cannot work for NEET. Using [IAF to deliver question papers] to testing centers indicates that no institution in India is free from bureaucratic bungling. Some basic reforms are necessary to eliminate the stranglehold of bureaucratic structure: 
- Reform the processes to make them simple, remove ambiguities, and make them technically sound
- Withdraw the over-riding power where technicalities outweigh the capabilities of bureaucrats.
- Allow lateral entry of technical experts to administrative pyramids to handle focused sensitive domains.
   
It is a band-aid approach when what the body needs is surgery. The military is for defense, not for civil logistics issues like the delivery of NEET question papers. It is high time to reform and revamp bureaucracy.
