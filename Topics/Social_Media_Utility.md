## Governing Social Media as Public Utility

An interesting [governance framework for social media has been proposed in the October issue of CACM](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/governing-social-media-as-a-public-utility/). It analyzes the efficacy of governing social media as a Public Utility like water, electricity, and telephone networks. Before we jump to any conclusion about whether such a governance model could be imposed on social media, let us examine it in a bit of detail. 

Although Public Broadcasting Systems, the Internet, and Telephone works are regulated by equity, quality, and universal access, there appears to be very little control in the governance of social media. But the question is, do we need governance? What is the purpose of governance? It will mean we want to
- Provide sufficient institutional safeguards to protect safety and privacy,
- Prevent autocratic expansion.

We do welcome the expansion, as it provides a convenient public forum for issues that affect citizens. On the other hand, anonymity in public expression can be used for harmful content. It boils down to two conflicting stakes in public life:
- Freedom of expression
- Freedom from harmful expansion.
