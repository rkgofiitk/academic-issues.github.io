## AI Use in Surveillance & Defense

Pentagon is in for an extended fight with Anthropic for control over deployment of Claude in surveillance and defense. Apparently, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is not averse to Pentagon's use of Claude for military purpose. Indeed it has a contract with Pentagon. However, the sticking point is the principled stand of against the surveillance on American citizens. It is nobody's concern that governments across the globe use sophisticated electronic tools for illegal surveillance, eavesdropping and intelligence gathering. About 1.5 million IP enabled cameras are deployed over the public spaces in 15 major cities in India. In an average 83 cameras keep 24/7 watch over 1000 persons in Hyderabad. Even the second tier cities like Indore compete with Delhi for high density of video and audio surveillance. Hyderabad, Delhi and Chennai are among the globally most surveilled cities in the world. Over 91% of vedio cameras in India are concentrated in four cities in India Hyderabad, Delhi, Chennai and Indore. Interestingly, Bangaluru does not figure among these cities. It has approximately a little under half a million video cameras successfully geotagged by the city police department. Until recently, Bangaluru Police personnels were not wearing bodycams. Now it has been made mandatory. So, if the police cameras are not picking up your mannerism (including when and how pick your nose) then you must be either moving in area under ill deployed or nonfunctional cameras or you have exceptional capabilities to move stealthly. If latter is the case then, perhaps, you should share your expertise with HAL for building stealth aircrafts. 

How AI changes the whole game of official surveillance?  Already, the government has biometric data of 1.34 billion people in Aadhar database. Additionally, telecom companies have KYC data if nearly 0.80 billion people. Any financial agency such as banks and insurance companies hold tons and tons of public data. Nearly 2.5 billion transactions occurs every month.  With high volume of data, training AI models is possible for every possible scenario and multi-lingual Indic languages. India AI-Kosh repository contains 5700 data-sets. 

AI with computer vision capabilities will narrow down search and assist law enforcement to extract a "black cat out a coal cellar" without having to go on a wild goose chase. AI assisted real-time facial recognition is used in public spaces.  Vehicle license plate is tracked in real-time through AI-automated traffic violation and management system. Data analytics on crowd density and movement can predict a flash crowd in public spaces using AI assisted high resolution computer vision tools. These tools may even flag anamoly in crowd behavior pattern to assist security aparatus.  AI systems have a certain threshold of accuracy, so the conventional human management and handling would be a necessity. However, the exploration space for focused search becomes a small.   While the positive side is understandable, the big brother watching becomes a reality. Governments thrive because of incessant divisive discords among people. Engineering a discord among people and groups by organizing gatherings is what political parties do day in and day out. However, the use of AI model will make this simpler and more effective. The use of AI models in public surveillance could give an edge in managing public perception by the party in power. It is likely become a "soft tactical nuke" in demolishing a genuine oppositions to the government.
