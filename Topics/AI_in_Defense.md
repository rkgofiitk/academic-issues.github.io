## AI Use in Surveillance & Defense

Pentagon is in for an extended fight with Anthropic for control over deployment of Claude in surveillance and defense. Apparently, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is not averse to Pentagon's use of Claude for military purpose. Indeed it has a contract with Pentagon. However, the sticking point is the principled stand of surveillance on American citizens.t is nobody's concern that governments across the globe use sophisticated electronic tools for illegal surveillance, eavesdropping and intelligence gathering. About 1.5 million IP enabled cameras are deployed over the public spaces in 15 major cities in India. In an average 83 cameras keep 24/7 watch over 1000 persons in Hyderabad. Even the second tier cities like Indore compete with Delhi for high density of video and audio surveillance. Hyderabad, Delhi and Chennai are among the most globally surveilled cities in the world. Over 91% of vedio cameras in India are concentrated in four cities in India Hyderabad, Delhi, Chennai and Indore. Interestingly, Bangaluru does not figure among these cities. It has approximately a little over half million video cameras successfully geotagged by the city police department. Until recently, Bangaluru Police personnels were not wearing bodycams. So, if the police cameras are not picking up your mannerism (including when and how pick your nose) then you must be either moving in area under ill deployed or nonfunctional cameras or you have exceptional capabilities to move stealthly. Perhaps, you should share your expertise with HAL for building stealth aircrafts. 

How AI changes the whole game of official surveillance? AI with computer vision capabilities will narrow down search and assist law enforcement to extract a "black cat out a coal cellar" without having to go on a wild goose chase. AI assisted real-time facial recognition is used in public spaces. Vehicle license plate is tracked in real-time through AI-automated traffic violation and management system. Data analytics on crowd density and movement can predict gathering of flash crowd in public spaces using AI assisted high resolution computer vision tools. These tools may even flag anamoly in crowd behavior pattern to assist security aparatus.  AI systems have a certain threshold of accuracy, so the conventional human management and handling should be a necessity. However, the exploration space for focused search becomes a small space.   While the positive side is understandable, the big brother watching becomes a reality. Government thrive because of incessant divisive discords among people.
