# AI First Approach

[Blog Index](../index.md)

It is the second part of topic ["Can AI Write 90% of Code"](./AI_coding.md). CEO of Shopify argues for a reverse approach
to introduce use of AI tools. In an [internal memo](https://www.msn.com/en-in/money/news/ai-is-replacing-jobs-shopify-ceo-says-prove-ai-can-t-do-the-job-before-hiring-someone/ar-AA1CuwdX?ocid=winp2fptaskbar&cvid=181d108cd0c94aee979098b07076db56&ei=5) He tells 
that before hiring a person for a job "Prove that AI cannot do it." 

I think one needs to learn a bit about the Shopify platform to understand the thinking behind the Shopify CEO's comment. 
Shopify is ecommerce platform where one can buy and sell. It supports enterprise solutions for B2B, B2C and Retails. There 
is difference between Amazon and Shopify. Amazon is an online <i>market place</i>. It can be viewed as a big shopping mall
where buyers and sellers meet to transact business. Shopify does not provide features of a shopping mall. It provides a 
platform like a city's infrastructure in a shopping district. One may sets a shop and store including inventory, accounting,
tax, payment and customer management system. So, Shopify can process sales, run stores and help grow business. One can start
a global business website over the platform. In other words, it provides tools and products rolled into one unified system to
help an enterprise sell to people everywhere. Therefore, Shopify platform is more complex than Amazon. It is difficult to 
build reputation and establish business on Shopify. Over Amazon sellers compete among themselves and, therefore, it becomes
easier for a seller to build reputation by providing excellent product and services. 

Considering the Shopify's line of operations, one thing becomes clear that it appears to have a client base that is technically
and resource-wise more richer than Amazon's client base. Therefore, Shopify can take a bit of relexed approach to update its
services than Amazon may do. It gives Shopify more leg-space to work for system updates and services. Having said that, I not 
discounting the fact that Shopify platform is much more complex software system. If one carefully goes through the published
part of the internal memo of Shopify, it is clear that the CEO recognizes the fact that employees must graduate to ask right
question to come to the clusion that a hiring is necessary. In a way he is urging the employees to learn about the technalities of
prompt engineering. So, that itself is a give away, the Shopify CEO essentially saying the employees to learn and train to
use AI tools. My observation is that the author of who published the internal memo has given a free publicity space to Shopify
CEO. Simply telling employees to train on AI tools would not have attracted the attention the deliberatedly leaked internal
memo has accomplished.

My intention of writing this post is to make the readers aware that the hype around AI assisted generative tools have two sides.
Many serious watchers of incorporating AI tools in government business may have learned that RBI has created a responsible AI
Group for standardizing the use of AI tools for its activities.  The prefix "responsible use" is significant. The current RBI
Governor holds a B. Tech in CSE from IIT Kanpur. The Chairperson of the Responsible AI Group is reknowned NLP researcher and
a Professor of IITB. He is also an alumni of IIT Kanpur. In a business of large volume of money transactions any small missetps
potentially create divasting calamitous effects on country's economy. UPI and other micro payment services NPCI is working 
fine, but RBI attempt to introduce digital rupee has not been launched after the pilot was run for a few days. I lost Rs 6.00
during the pilot run of e-rupee. I am not sure if many people had similar experience, but there could be a problem in use of 
software tools, specially, privacy and security aspects.
