# AI First Approach

[Blog Index](../index.md)

It is the second part of topic ["Can AI Write 90% of Code"](./AI_coding.md). CEO of Shopify argues a reverse approach
to introduce use of AI tools. In an [internal memo](https://www.msn.com/en-in/money/news/ai-is-replacing-jobs-shopify-ceo-says-prove-ai-can-t-do-the-job-before-hiring-someone/ar-AA1CuwdX?ocid=winp2fptaskbar&cvid=181d108cd0c94aee979098b07076db56&ei=5) He tells that before hiring a person for a job "Prove
that AI cannot do it." We need to understand the shopify platform. It is ecommerce platform where one can buy
and sell. It supports enterprise solutions for B2B, B2C and Retails. There is difference between Amazon and
Shopify. Amazon is an online market place like a big shopping mall. Shopify gives a platform on which you can set
a stand-alone business enterprise, handle and manage business related activities. Shopify can process sales, 
run stores and help grow business. One can start a global business website over the platform. In other words, it
provides tools and products rolled into one unified system to help an enterprise sell to people everywhere. 
Therefore, Shopify platform is more complex than Amazon. It is difficult to build reputation and establish business 
on Shopify. Over Amazon sellers compete among themselves but easier to build reputation by assured services.
