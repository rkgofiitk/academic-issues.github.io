## Taxing Data & Virtually Internet

[Blog Index](../index.md)

A [new report of research innovation on tax collection](https://www.msn.com/en-in/money/technology/data-usage-in-india-could-soon-get-taxed-what-it-means-for-end-users/ar-AA1YpHsi?ocid=winp2fptaskbar&cvid=c8eafc6f56da4ec3a0ba19f92a4a6964&ei=30) by the Indian Bureaucrats is quite remarkable!! The idea originated from Telecom companies, which have consistently resorted to hike in the rates for value-added services over the past few years. According to revenue experts, even a modest tax rate of INR 1 per GB could generate annual revenue of INR 230 billion (exceeding USD 23 billion). The proposed tax will have wide-ranging effects impacting banking and UPI transactions, transport logistics, travel, hotel booking, e-commerce, remote meetings and conferences, nearly every aspect of daily life. It is a brilliant way to implement a censorship, as creating  reels on platforms like YouTube, facebook, instagram, or posting on X will be subject to a tax. Voice over IP comes under it. So, if you are using whatsup call service, the duration of call will be subject to tax.  It raises concern whether the Bureaucrats will eventually decide to tax even regular voice calls.
