## AI Impact Summit at Bharat Mandap

[Blog Index](../index.md)

The AI Impact Summit was a significant event at Bharat Mandapam last week, with Satya Nadella, Sam Altman, and Sundar Pichai among the influential attendees. NVIDIA's CEO canceled his trip to the summit, reportedly due to a bug he caught during weeks of travels prior to summit. However, the presence of AI trinity Satya(m) S(hiv)am, and Sundar in India was a rare event of the week. Three [sovereign LLM models](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/india-rolls-out-three-sovereign-ai-models-sarvam-ai-gnani-ai-bharatgen-to-take-on-big-tech-13834672.html) were rolled out during the summit. Sarvam.ai released 30B and 105B LLMs, fully trained in India, for reasoning, coding, and agentic AI, claiming better performance than DeepSeek R1 and Gemini Flash. Gnani.ai offers voice AI that can clone voices in 12 Indian languages with only 10 seconds of audio. Notwithstanding the two viral bits of drama ([Galgotia mess up](https://www.instagram.com/p/DU9Y8MYDlFW/) and [Shirtless protest](https://www.businesstoday.in/bt-tv/whats-hot/video/ai-summit-chaos-pm-modis-sharp-attack-on-congress-over-shirtless-protest-517522-2026-02-23)) played out in mainstream media, let us understand India's push to acquire sovereignty in AI and Agentic AI.

[BharatGen](https://bharatgen.com/bharatgen-param2-ai-launch-india/) is a 17TB Param Indic Language generative AI model targeting healthcare, governance, and fintech, education, and agriculture. BharatGen is a primarily research-based consortium of academic institutions led by IIT Bombay. While the model may not have the same level of user experience, it will eventually spawn many incubations and startups for multilayer innovations in sovereign AI models, especially in targeted sectors.  

[Gnani.AI](https://www.gnani.ai/) specializes in speech-to-text conversion, which is reported to be well-suited for AI bots in human interaction across 9 languages. It will be ideally suited for complaint resolution and query resolution via interactive voice bots. The capabilities may eventually turn into an Agentic tool working as voice-enabled AI assistants on the go. 

[Sarvam.AI](https://www.sarvam.ai/models/) is reported to be special for the Indian AI ecosystem. Nandan Nilekani is a high profile mentor, advisor and supporter of Sarvam. It does not match ChatGPT or Gemini's generic benchmarks, but focuses on core problems such as token efficiency, code-mixed speech, dialect noise, and OCRs of messy Indian documents. Creating a sovereign stack on these issues will eventually make Sarvam a dependable backbone for developing many SMLs and agentic AI tools for numerous indic-based case deployments. The key innovators under the hood at Sarvam.AI, Dr. Vivek Raghavan and Dr. Pratyush Kumar, have years of research and academic experience. Dr. Pratyush Kumar also serves as an adjunct faculty at IIT Madras. Dr. Raghavan was a chief biometric architect for Aadhar, having more than 12 years of experience with UIADIA. The innovators have over 20 years of experience between them.   

India received a massive thumbs-up from world leaders in AI, with a promise of a 250 billion USD investment opportunity for building AI infrastructure. These include massive data centers, cloud platforms, and the overall AI ecosystem. Andhra Pradesh has already cornered proposals for data center commitments from Google at Vizag and a 1200 qubit Quantum Data Centers at Amravati. Another QDC is also proposed at NOIDA. The Amravati QDC is envisoned as India's first global hub for quantum technology with partnership of IBM, TCS, L&T.

The ugly side of India's political spectrum was also in display at the international event. A Shirtless protest was organized  by a handful of middle-aged congress activists masquerding as GenZ anger against the PM. The attendees faced massive chaos  on the opening day of the summit. Indian [IT Minister offered apologized](https://www.thehindu.com/videos/watch-ashwini-vaishnaw-apologises-for-disruptions-at-india-ai-summit-kharge-labels-event-utter-chaos/article70646205.ece) to delegates. However, a [NOIDA based private university (Galgotia University) created a major controversy](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/more-embarrassment-for-galgotias-university-after-robodog-in-house-soccer-drone-from-korea-called-out/articleshow/128502877.cms) by claiming that a Chinese robot dog and a Korean Soccer Drone were its in-house developments. The University's chief communicator claimed they have plans to invest over INR 3.5 billion (equivalent to 41-42 million USD) in AI infrastructure. Apparently, the University was allotted a bigger Booth than IITs for the display of its exhibits. Someone in MEITY should be held responsible for the fiasco. How could the exhibits of Galgoria University pass the filters to acquire a substantial exposure based on false claims? 

The academic dishonesty exhibited in the Galgotia incident at the AI summit, unfortunately, undermines the Indian academic landscape. UGC and other academic regulatory bodies are controlled by ineffective leadership within the Ministry of Education. It is time for the GoI to take academics seriously. Merit and merit alone should be the basic requirement for scholars, and the leadership should refrain from caste-based politics to pull the country back into the dark ages of mediocrity.
