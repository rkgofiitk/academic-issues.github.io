# Erastosthenese Sieve

I learned there is an AICTE initiative "AICTE Technical Book Writing and Translation" to create engineering text in twelve 
Indian languages. Anna University in Tamilnadu has been using local language instruction in engineering for 10 years now.
However, the uptick for tehcnical course registrations in vernacular language is very low. National Education Policy (NEP)
states that "wherever possible the medium of instruction until at least class 5, but preferably till class 8 and beyond, will be
home language/ mother-tongue/ local language/ regional language."

Having created the context, let me reveal the intent behind this post. I received a copy of bangla article on "Erastosthenes Sieve" 
from Prof Ashok Mallick on the eve of "bangla navabarsha." As usual Prof Mallick's article reveals his deep knowledge and interest 
in popularizing mathematics among vernacular readers. It is debatable how many of bengali readers really read technical 
articles in bangla. However, it is good to keep an open mind about technical contents written in local language. I doubt if
there are many comparable technical articles in English by Indian authors that parallels Prof. Mallick's writing on mathmatics
in bangla. Let me add a caveat here. I have now knowledge if Prof Mallick is a participating author in AICTE initiative for technical 
book writing project.

My point is there is a necessity of creating outstanding technical texts in vernacular language as English becomes a non-sustainable
link language. There are enough reasons for it. First of all, as a country with long civilizational history India never really
adopted English as the home language like other colonies of Britain.

[Erastosthenes of Cyrene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eratosthenes) was an ancient Greek mathematicians of third century BC. That was
nearly two thousand four hundred years ago. Specialization did not exist 2400 years ago. Erastosthenes had varied interests that range 
from mathematics, geography, astronomy, to music and poetry. He was the first person to have calculated
- The Earth's circumference with less than 1% error
- The Earth's "axial tilt" with similar accuracy.
- The Earth's projection using the concept of parallels and Merdians.

Cryene was founded by Greek and located in Libya. Speaking technically he was a Lybian by birth and lived in Alexandria from 245 BC till his
death. However, he studied in Athenes under the Greek philosopher [Aristo of Chios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristo_of_Chios). 
Another celebrated ancient Greek mathematician [Euclid](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Euclid/) work
<i>Elements</i> appeared about 300 BC. In book IX of Elements, Euclid proved that infinitely many prime numbers exists. Another important
result that Euclid proved is about prime factorization. There was gap of nearly 50 year after Euclid's work on prime numbers until 
Erastosthenes created a simple algorithm for finding prime numbers.
