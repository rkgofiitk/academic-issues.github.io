## AI Internship Without a Degree

Excelling in creative arts and literature does not require a degree. Rabindranath Tagore did not have a degree. However, [Tagore was trained extensively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabindranath_Tagore) wothout a formal channel of learning and education like a college or school. Agusta Ada, who was first to realize that Charles Babbage's mechanical general purpose machine did have applications beyond calculations, also not have any formal training in algorithm, mathematics or calculus. In recent times, school dropouts like Bill Gates, or Mark Zukerberg made fortunes creating big tech companies like Microsoft and Meta. The point is creativity cannot be taught, excellence can only be nurtured but not raised. Education is for creating assembly line averages to make a living. Yet we talk about good schools and institutions not entirely without a reason. Greate institutions trigger the sleeping talent and unleashes them to full potentials. A recent news item about offer of an [internship by an Indian AI startup](https://www.ndtv.com/offbeat/indian-ceo-offers-rs-2-lakh-per-month-internship-no-degree-needed-even-high-schoolers-welcome-9039178) has ignited some interest in business world. Mr. Siddharth Bhatia, CEO of Puch AI, posted internship offer on X. The post says the internship offer is open to highschool students and fully remote, allowing flexibility to work from anywhere in India. Anyone interested can directly get in tocuh with Mr. Bhatia. 

In this context, let me narrage an anecdote concerning creative literature when we were growing up in a small town in eastern part of India. Our friends were whishpering about making a few bucks for assignments of translating few pages of English to a vernacular language. Apparently, the pages were torn from books of ancient little known english novels or story books and randomly distributed to the recruited translators. The rules about copyright and plagiarism not so stringent in those days. The rumour was that translated pages were compiled and re-edited in form of novels and stories by a celebrated author in the vernacular language. It is difficult for us either to confirm or reject the rumour at this point of time. However, there were some striking similarities in works of the said author with a few random classical English literature. 

Though Mr. Siddharth Bhatia's offer of internship look very different from the anecdote mentioned above, the similarity is in crowd engagement for garnering the talent. The published news items or Mr. Bhatia's post are not quite forthcoming on the issue of patents or royalities accruing out of the work of interns. The news stories only are highlighting two points about the offer of internship
- No degree is needed for internship offer in AI
- The amount of compensation in the internship
- The opportunities for remote work.
