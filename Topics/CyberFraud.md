## Handling Cyber Fraud

Indian government has a portal for reporting cyber fraud. Citizens would like to know about the experiences and simplicity of reporting cyber fraud on that channel. The issue here is that of "chicken and egg." If someone is a victim of cyber fraud, most likely it may happen through the handheld device like phone, or Ipad and via cellular channel. Once the SIM cloned or the device is hijacked due to installation of a rouge app, there is no way to use the SIM or the device. Interestingly, Cyber Fraud portal insists on phone number, details of timing, channel on which the fraud happened, aadhar card, and many other personal details including photographs or documents proving the fraud. Quite obviously, the documents can be extracted only through two-factor authentication for any financial fraud. It implies that there is no way to know self-extract the documents. So, only other way of procuring the documents is approaching the bank or relevant financial custodians. Hence, the victim loses the vital time between occurrence of event and the reporting and the fraudster gains the advtange of time.

The other option is to approach the police. It also asks all the forementioned details. If an ordinary citizens were to collect these documents or anciliary proofs, then it will take at least 6-8 hours of precious time. The window of time between actual occurrence of event and the reporting is more than sufficient for the fraudster to cover the track and vanish. Now about the victim's harassment aftermath the fraud. Especially, in the case of unauthorized transactions, the victim must block all the possible channels for any subsequent unauthorized transactions. It will mean electronic transactions must be stopped.  Blocking electronic transaction can be done through handheld devices. But if the device itself is hacked then the victim should physically approach the custodian. That puts additional delay in the process. Sometimes, the officials won't place a block unless the victim submits identity proofs like Aadhar and PAN. Therefore, the blocking process may not be easy as it may sound. 

The second issue in the victim's harassment begins to unfold after the victim's device is sanitized completely. The bank will require KYC update and it may take up to 3 days for restoration of transaction rights. Most banks are migrating to app based INB transactions. App based INB is optimized for use in mobile devices, it uses biometrics (finger prints) or face IDs for enhanced security while web-based INB offer broader user interface for more extended services. App-based INB becomes useless for a hacked device. While web-based INB may work, but not without a sanitized phone. However, the victim must go through a series of intervention from the relevent branch of the bank before the resumption of services as explained above. Both have advantages and disadvantages as shown in the table below.  
|--------|---------|-------|
| Features | App-based INB | Web-based INB|
|--------|---------|--------|
|User experience | Smart design with touch friendly use | Text or Icon based switches for use|
|--------|---------|--------|
| Updates frequency | High | Moderately low|
|--------|---------|--------|
| Dependency | Device dependent | Can be accessed from any where and any device|
|--------|---------|--------|
| Connecity | Operates in low data connectivity | Rquires stable N/W connectivity|
|--------|---------|--------|
| Performance | Optimized for device | Could time out when connection breaks|
|--------|---------|--------|

The feature of frequent updates for app-based INB might be hidden curse if the design flow is not very strictly verified. Typically, the app designer use hardware and software codesign approach conforming to RBI's Digital Payment Security Controls and SEBI’s Cyber Resilience Framework so that they can use device biometric sensors, SIM bindings, and secure keystores. Cryptographic keys are stored in h/w and not in app's storage.  I am not sure if the apps use AI backed anomaly detections.

Most frauds in India happen through SIM swappings and mobile malware installations. So, hardware and software co-design involving device binding, biometrics and secure keystore is the best approach to prevent fraud even if the SIM is compromized. SBI YONO's hardware layer Android keystore/IoS secure enclave to store cryptographic key. Its software layer uses end-to-end encrption for transactions. I am not sure if SBI's YONO uses AI-aided fraud detection logic. HDFC is about the release its app-based INB. The bank uses its own Trusted Execution Environment (TEE, a sandbox environment) for secure PIN and biometric processing. App is based on AI-driven fraud logic.
