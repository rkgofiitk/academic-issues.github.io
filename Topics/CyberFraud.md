## Handling Cyber Fraud

Indian government has a portal for reporting cyber fraud. Citizens would like to know about the experiences and simplicity of reporting cyber fraud on that channel. The issue is that of chicken and egg. If someone is a victim of cyber fraud, most likely it happened through the handheld device like phone, or Ipad and through cellular channel. Once SIM cloned or a device is hijacked due to installation of a rouge app, there is no way to use the SIM or the device. However, Cyber Fraud portal insists on phone number, details of timing, channel on which fraud happened, aadhar card, and many other personal details including photographs or documents proving the fraud. The police dept also asks all the forementioned details. If an ordinary citizens were to collect these documents or anciliary proofs, then it will take at least 6-8 hours of precious time. The window of time between actual occurrence of event and the reporting is more than sufficient for the fraudster to cover the track and vanish.
