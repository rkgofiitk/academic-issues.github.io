## Voter's Identity

ECI carried out an onerous task of SIR across 12 states and also successfully conducted election process in four states, Assam, West Bengal, Tamilnadu and Puducherry. It is a commendable and perhaphs most thankless task in India. Conduction incident free election in West Bengal is indeed an outstanding job. However, our theme of discussion here is not about how to ensure violance free election, but how to ensure free-and-fair election with minimum deployment security forces and other human resources.

[Electroinc Voting Machines were introduced in phased manner from 1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_voting_in_India). Prior to EVM ballot papers were used in Indian election like it is still in USA and many other supposedly advanced nations.  Let me digress a nit here to trace the genesis of the concept of election, and voting. It dates back to 508 BC and most historians think it originated from Ancient Greece. In fact, etymological origin of election is linked to Greek word "eklektos." Ir means act of choosing, picking or selecting from a set of options. The process which facilitates the picking or choosing is known as voting. The older Greek method of election relied on show of hands. In Ancient India, the evidence of [non-monarchial governments](https://www.infinityfoundation.com/mandala/h_es/h_es_muhlb_democra_frameset.htm) goes back to Vedic times. However, republicanism in India was practiced vigorously during fifth and sixth centuries BC when the civialization was gradually turning to urbanization. The earliest Buddhist scripture "[Pali Cannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali_Canon)" gave a sufficiently detailed dscription of Vaisali as an urban settlement of parks, ponds, and planned buildings for human settlements. Even Panini work [Astadhyayi](https://ashtadhyayi.com/), Mahabharata, [Jain Cannon](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Jaina-canon), and [Katuliya's Arthasasthra](https://archive.org/details/in.gov.ignca.900dealt) with ancient republicanism in India. The [Pandyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandya_dynasty), the [Pallavs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty), the [Cholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_dynasty), and the [Vijaynagar kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire) had [the practice of seeking collective opinions from Sabhas](https://www.infinityfoundation.com/mandala/h_es/h_es_muhlb_democra_frameset.htm) in resolving issues and making administrative policy decisions. The Sabhas of ancient kingdoms were chosen from village communities. So while the voting and elections were largely well-established means of reaching consensus that affect large population, the modern method of electronic electioneering did not evolve fully until 1980s. The [first general elections in India were held in 1951-52]([https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951%E2%80%9352](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951%E2%80%9352_Indian_general_election). The futility of ballot paper paper based election became transparent to voters with large-scale [scientific rigging](https://grokipedia.com/page/scientific_rigging). Riggings defeat the very purpose of election and robs the basic rights of voters. It forced ECI to look for alternative to  ballot papers based voting. 

EVMs are simple standalone tamper-proof electronic voting machines which accoumulated tally of votes cast by the voters. However, unscruplous political parties soon outsmarted ECI and developed various means of scientific rigging with EVM. Politcal Action Companies sprang up to implement the process of rigging EVMs with asistance from Polling Officers, Police and Oversight teams. On the top of it ECI carried out [Intensive Rivision of Voters Rolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Intensive_Revision) in 2002 and then in 2025 just before Bihar elections.
