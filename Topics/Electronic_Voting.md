## Electronic Voting System & Voter's Identity 

ECI carried out an onerous task of SIR across 12 states and also successfully conducted election process in four states, Assam, West Bengal, Tamilnadu and Puducherry. It is a commendable and perhaphs most thankless task in India. Conduction incident free election in West Bengal is indeed an outstanding job. However, our theme of discussion here is not about how to ensure violance free election, but how to ensure free-and-fair election with minimum deployment security forces and other human resources.

[Electroinc Voting Machines were introduced in phased manner from 1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_voting_in_India). Prior to introduction of EVMs, ballot papers were used in Indian elections like it is still in USA and many other supposedly advanced nations.  Let me digress a nit here to trace the genesis of the concept of election, and voting to understand why elections and votings remain a time tested means to acquire legitimacy to govern. 

Election dates back to 508 BC and most historians think it originated from Ancient Greece. The etymological origin of election is linked to Greek word "eklektos." Ir means act of choosing, picking or selecting from a set of options. The process which facilitates the picking or choosing is known as voting. The older Greek method of election relied on show of hands. In Ancient India, the evidence of [non-monarchial governments](https://www.infinityfoundation.com/mandala/h_es/h_es_muhlb_democra_frameset.htm) goes back to Vedic times. However, republicanism in India was practiced vigorously during fifth and sixth centuries BC when the civialization progressively realized importance of civic amenties and urbanization for comforts of living. The earliest Buddhist scripture "[Pali Cannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali_Canon)" gave a sufficiently detailed dscription of Vaisali as an urban settlement of parks, ponds, and planned buildings for human settlements. Panini's work [Astadhyayi](https://ashtadhyayi.com/), Mahabharata, [Jain Cannon](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Jaina-canon), and [Katuliya's Arthasasthra](https://archive.org/details/in.gov.ignca.900) dealt with ancient republicanism in India. The [Pandyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandya_dynasty), the [Pallavs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty), the [Cholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_dynasty), and the [Vijaynagar kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire) had [the practice of seeking collective opinions from Sabhas](https://www.infinityfoundation.com/mandala/h_es/h_es_muhlb_democra_frameset.htm) in resolving conflicting issues and making administrative policy decisions. The Sabhas of ancient kingdoms were chosen from village communities. So while the voting and elections were largely well-established means of reaching consensus that affect large population, the modern method of electronic electioneering in India did not evolve fully until 1980s. The [first general elections in India were held in 1951-52]([https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951%E2%80%9352](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951%E2%80%9352_Indian_general_election). The futility of ballot paper paper based election became transparent to citizens when large-scale [scientific rigging](https://grokipedia.com/page/scientific_rigging) became a reality during the period 1970 to 1990. Riggings defeat the very purpose of election and robs the basic rights of citizens. There were may form of election rigging, such as, 
- Booth capturing
- Intimidiation of election officials
- Source jamming
- Slow counting and tampering of tabulations
- Physical theft of ballot boxes
- Invalid votes
- Casting votes of absent and dead voters

Political parties organized local muscle men to orchestrate election rigging. The basic approach was to scare away the genunine voters and cast votes on their behalf. Most of these rigging methods work even with EVMs. UP, Bihar and West Bengal gained notoriety in gaming elections.

EVMs are simple standalone tamper-proof electronic voting machines which accoumulated tally of votes cast by the voters. It eliminated only three things, viz., invalid votes, slow counting and physical theft of ballots.  However, rest of the rigging techniques largely remained in tact with support of a strong cadre of muscle men. Politcal Action Companies sprang up to organize the process of rigging EVMs with asistance from Polling Officers, Police and Oversight teams. So, ECI has to rely on large posse of security forces to ward off threats from large scale election related violences across India. In the recently concluded elections of four states ECI adopted several strong measure to ensure free and fair elections.

ECI has provisions for updating voters rolls on regular basis. However, the voter rolls were only for addition of new voters. In 2025 just before Bihar elections, ECI carried out a Special Intensive Rivision of voter list. The last [Intensive Rivision of Voters Rolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Intensive_Revision) was conducted in 2002 . There is a gap of 23 years in between the two SIRs. ECI relied on AI and other modern software to flag many logical discrepancies. The political parties instead of assisting ECI went to supreme court to delay and derail the SIR process. Since stakes in elections are very high, ECI need to carry out SIR in every five years. Still the sanctity of voters list remain a hotly contested subject. The crux of the contest lies in authenticacy of voters and ability to cast votes in voilence free fair atmosphere. How to ensure it?

Voter's identity and casting votes cannot be more important than ensuring authenticity of financial transcations, property deals,  entries in Airport, defence and other sensitive establisments.  We have a well-established biometrics-based robust protocol for the latter. Why cannot voting and elections be turned into an entirely AI-based automated process? Let us analyse the proposal a bit in depth. 

Billions of digital payments are made every day. As of early 2026, 660 million UPI transactions are made every day. The value of these transaction top 10 Billion US dollar a day. Besides, UPI other digital modes like NEFT, IMPS, RTGs, Fast Tag average over 12 millions a day. RBI reports an average of Rs. 1.40 as disputed or fraudulent transaction per hundered one thousand rupees. Total loss is estimated as 2.5 billion rupees (approximately 261 million US dollars) annually which is a substantial amount. The detection of cyber criminals remains a challenge, but the efficiency and convenience of digital payement system outweigh the losses. Counterfeiting and untraceable fradulent transactions were areas of major concern in physical payement system that existed prior to digital payment system. Though it is difficult to give a proper estimate due to untraceable transactions, trillions are lost tax revenue, a saving of 520 million USD in currency management, and a shadow economy worth nearly a quarter of India's total productivity almost crippled Indian economy.

Let us now spend a bit to examine the impacts of biometric-based autthentication of property rights  is now mandatory across India. It has unlocked about 2% of frozen land assets contributing to overall GDP of the country. It has made benami transaction almost impossible if has completely eliminated the menace of nexus between politicians and land mafia. Furthermore, the process empowered small landowner to claim settlement of compensation when the government acquires land for infrastructure development or large CAP projects. Block-chain based property management has also reduced litigation issues, faster and enhanced accesses to loans and mortgage. Coupled with smart contract the forzen land assets are turned into growth engine. In fact, property related litigations account for 66% of civil cases across Indian courts. 

The evolution of India's decomcratic structure of election and voting has been a complex case study for any avid student of emancipation of people's rights and accountability of political dispensation. From the large scale incidents of booth capturing, source jamming, denying voters accessibility to free choices through bribes, coercion, intimidiation every reform has protected voters and raised the importance of election that in recent Bengal elections the voting percentage topped 90% of the electortate. AI-driven system backed by biometric-based authentication has served well in finance, banking, insurance, stock markets, property dealings, serveillance based secured entries to sensitive places including airports. The secured electronic ecosystem has deservedly earned people's fait. So, the elections --  the very foundation of democratic legitmacy -- requires the trusted and test  why can't the election and voting process be elevated to next level of electronic upgradation? Voters can cast and authenticate their votes using hand-held devices, ATMS, or upgraded EVMs. The requirement of election officials can be reduced, deployment of security forces can be brought down substantially. I am not saying that it will completely eliminate the role of muscle men in election. But they will find it harder to rig the system. In fact, any electronically secured system can be hacked but the time effort to engineer such a hacking makes it easier to discover.
